📖 Our Story

🤝 Built to Bridge the Gap

China's digital advertising market is massive — but for international companies, it's also opaque, bureaucratic, and hard to navigate. We built Baidu PPC Pro to change that.

As a Baidu second-tier reseller connected directly to Baidu official and first-tier agents, we occupy a unique position in the ecosystem. We're not a middleman adding layers — we're the bridge that makes the system work for companies outside China.

Our team is based in Shanghai with over 25 years of combined experience in Chinese digital advertising. We've helped 200+ companies from 10+ countries open Baidu accounts, fund campaigns, and navigate the compliance landscape — all in English.

By leveraging AI for campaign management and optimization, we operate efficiently and pass those savings to you in the form of lower service fees — without compromising on quality.
