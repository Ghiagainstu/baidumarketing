바이두의 2026년 에이전트 시대: AI가 광고를 만드는 새로운 시대

May 13, 2026 약 4분 Platform Baidu PPC Pro 팀

On May 13-14, 2026, Baidu held its annual Create developer conference in Beijing. The theme was "AI Self-Evolution." But for international advertisers, the real headline is this: Baidu is no longer just a search engine that sells ads. It is becoming an AI agent platform — and the way businesses interact with Chinese consumers is changing fast.

We attended the keynotes and product demos. Here is what matters for overseas brands.

🤖 The Big Picture: From Chatbots to Agents

Baidu's CEO Robin Li introduced a new concept at the conference: DAA — Daily Active Agents. Just as DAU (Daily Active Users) measured the mobile internet era, DAA measures how many AI agents are actively working and delivering results for users.

The shift is significant. Baidu is moving from "users search, we show ads" to "agents act, we deliver outcomes." For advertisers, this means the entry point for reaching Chinese consumers is expanding beyond the search box.

📱 DuMate: Baidu's Universal Agent

The biggest product launch was DuMate (百度搭子), Baidu's new universal AI agent. Think of it as a single interface that combines search, coding, research, data analysis, and app creation — all powered by Baidu's ERNIE models.

DuMate is available on both desktop and mobile, with real-time sync between devices. In benchmark tests, it achieved state-of-the-art results across multiple international evaluations.

💡
Insight: DuMate is not a chatbot. It is an agent that completes tasks — market research, competitive analysis, report generation — without requiring the user to manually piece together results. For international advertisers, this means Chinese buyers may soon be using AI agents to research and compare products before they ever click on a search ad.

🎬 Three More Agent Products

Baidu launched three additional agent products at the conference:

FaMou (伐谋 2.0)Decision AgentSelf-evolving decision-making for industrial scenarios: production scheduling, process optimization, logistics planning.

🧠 What This Means for International Advertisers

The Create 2026 announcements point to three shifts that overseas brands need to understand:

1. The Buyer Journey Is Changing

Chinese B2B and B2C buyers are increasingly using AI agents to research products, compare options, and even request quotes — all before interacting with a brand's advertising. This means your Baidu presence needs to be agent-friendly: structured data, clear product information, and landing pages that agents can parse.

2. Digital Humans Are Production-Ready

Baidu YiJing's upgrade means digital human content is no longer limited to short livestream clips. Brands can now create hours of interactive video content with AI-generated hosts. For overseas companies that cannot send a Chinese-speaking spokesperson to China, this is a cost-effective alternative.

3. Baidu's Cloud Is Now Agent Infrastructure

Baidu Cloud has been repositioned as "the new full-stack AI cloud for large-scale agent applications." This means the platform is optimizing for agent workloads — which will affect how ads are served, how leads are captured, and how campaigns are optimized.

⚠️
Important: These agent capabilities are integrated into Baidu's advertising ecosystem. To access them — DuMate for market research, YiJing for digital human content, MiaoDa for app creation — you need an active Baidu account with proper Chinese business credentials.

🚧 The Barrier for Overseas Companies

Every product announced at Create 2026 is built on Baidu's Chinese ecosystem. To use DuMate for business research, to deploy YiJing digital humans for your brand, or to run agent-optimized campaigns — you need a Baidu advertising account. And a Baidu advertising account requires:

  • A mainland Chinese business license
  • A Chinese bank account for billing
  • A Chinese phone number for verification
  • Local compliance and content review

Most international companies do not have these. This is the structural barrier between your brand and the most advanced AI marketing tools in China.
