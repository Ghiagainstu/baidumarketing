# The GEO Audit Checklist: 15 Questions to Test Your Brand's AI Visibility in China

You cannot improve what you cannot measure. Most international advertisers have no idea whether Chinese AI models know their brand exists — let alone whether they recommend it.

This 15-question audit will give you a clear picture of where you stand. Answer each question honestly, score your results, and you will know exactly where the gaps are.

## How to Use This Checklist

For each question, score yourself:
- **2 points:** Fully done and actively maintained
- **1 point:** Partially done or done but outdated
- **0 points:** Not done at all

**Scoring guide:**
- **25–30 points:** Strong GEO foundation — you are ahead of most competitors
- **15–24 points:** Moderate — some foundations in place, significant gaps remain
- **0–14 points:** Weak — Chinese AI models likely cannot find or recommend your brand

---

## Platform Presence (Questions 1–5)

**Q1. Do you have a Baidu Baike (百度百科) entry for your brand?**
The single most important entity signal for Baidu's AI.
- 2: Verified entry with accurate, comprehensive information
- 1: Entry exists but is outdated or incomplete
- 0: No entry

**Q2. Do you have an active Zhihu (知乎) presence?**
China's Q&A platform, heavily cited by all Chinese AI models.
- 2: Verified account with 20+ expert answers
- 1: Account exists but little activity
- 0: No presence

**Q3. Is your brand mentioned in Chinese media?**
AI models cite news articles and industry publications.
- 2: Regular mentions in relevant Chinese publications (3+ in last 6 months)
- 1: Occasional mentions (1–2 in last 6 months)
- 0: No Chinese media coverage

**Q4. Do you have a WeChat Official Account?**
Important for entity verification and content distribution.
- 2: Verified account with regular publishing
- 1: Account exists but inactive
- 0: No account

**Q5. Is your brand listed in Chinese business directories?**
Supporting entity signals for knowledge graph inclusion.
- 2: Listed in 3+ relevant directories
- 1: Listed in 1–2 directories
- 0: Not listed anywhere

---

## Content Quality (Questions 6–10)

**Q6. Do you have Chinese-language content that answers buyer questions?**
Content that AI models can quote directly.
- 2: Comprehensive FAQ, comparison tables, and buyer guides in Chinese
- 1: Basic product descriptions in Chinese
- 0: No Chinese content

**Q7. Does your Chinese content include data and statistics?**
AI models prefer data-rich content for citation.
- 2: Content includes benchmarks, specifications, and industry data
- 1: Some data points but not comprehensive
- 0: Marketing copy without data

**Q8. Is your website accessible and fast from China?**
AI models cannot cite content they cannot access.
- 2: Hosted in or near China, loads under 3 seconds
- 1: Accessible but slow (3–5 seconds)
- 0: Blocked or very slow from China

**Q9. Do you have comparison content (your brand vs alternatives)?**
Comparison tables are among the most-cited content types by AI.
- 2: Detailed comparison tables covering key decision criteria
- 1: Basic positioning without direct comparisons
- 0: No comparison content

**Q10. Do you have case studies with measurable results?**
AI models cite specific, verifiable outcomes.
- 2: Multiple case studies with specific metrics (cost savings, efficiency gains)
- 1: General success stories without specific numbers
- 0: No case studies

---

## AI Citation (Questions 11–15)

**Q11. When you ask ERNIE about your industry, does it mention your brand?**
Direct test of ERNIE visibility.
- 2: Brand mentioned by name with accurate description
- 1: Brand mentioned but with inaccurate or incomplete information
- 0: Not mentioned

**Q12. When you ask DeepSeek about your industry, does it mention your brand?**
Direct test of DeepSeek visibility.
- 2: Brand mentioned with data or citations
- 1: Brand mentioned vaguely
- 0: Not mentioned

**Q13. When you ask Doubao about your industry, does it mention your brand?**
Direct test of Doubao visibility.
- 2: Brand mentioned with context
- 1: Brand mentioned briefly
- 0: Not mentioned

**Q14. Do Chinese AI models cite your content as a source?**
Not just mentioning your brand, but linking to or quoting your content.
- 2: Your content is directly quoted or cited
- 1: Your brand is mentioned but content is not cited
- 0: Neither

**Q15. Are your competitors being cited by Chinese AI models?**
Understanding the competitive landscape in AI search.
- 2: You are cited more than competitors
- 1: Competitors are cited, you are not
- 0: Neither you nor competitors are cited (early market)

## Your Score and Next Steps

### 25–30: Strong Foundation
Your GEO is in good shape. Focus on deepening content, expanding to new AI platforms, and monitoring competitive shifts.

### 15–24: Moderate — Action Needed
You have some foundations but significant gaps exist. Prioritize the questions where you scored 0 or 1 — these are your highest-impact improvements.

**Immediate priorities:**
- Score 0 on Q1 (Baike): Create a Baidu Baike entry immediately
- Score 0 on Q2 (Zhihu): Establish a Zhihu presence and begin answering questions
- Score 0 on Q11–13 (AI citation): Focus on building the platform signals that feed AI models

### 0–14: Weak — Urgent Action Required
Chinese AI models likely cannot find or recommend your brand. You are invisible in a rapidly growing channel.

**Start here:**
1. Create a Baidu Baike entry (this alone can jump your score by 4–6 points)
2. Build a Chinese-language landing page accessible from China
3. Establish a Zhihu presence with 10 expert answers
4. Get at least one Chinese media placement
