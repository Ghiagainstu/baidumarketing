# Monitoring Your Brand in Chinese AI Search: Tools and Methods for 2026

You have invested in GEO. You have built your Baidu Baike entry, established a Zhihu presence, and created Chinese-language content. But how do you know if it is working? Unlike PPC, there is no dashboard that shows "AI impressions" or "AI clicks."

Monitoring your brand's presence in Chinese AI search requires a manual, systematic approach. Here is exactly how to do it.

## The Monthly AI Citation Audit

Once per month, ask each major Chinese AI model the same set of 15 questions about your industry. Record the results in a spreadsheet.

### The 15-Question Test Set

Create 15 questions that your target customers might ask. Structure them in three tiers:

**Tier 1 — Industry questions (5 questions):**
- "What are the best [product category] for [use case] in China?"
- "How do I choose a [product category] supplier in China?"
- "What certifications do I need for [product category] in China?"

**Tier 2 — Comparison questions (5 questions):**
- "[Your brand] vs [Competitor A] — which is better for [use case]?"
- "What are the differences between [your product type] and [alternative]?"
- "Which [product category] brands are most popular in China?"

**Tier 3 — Specific questions (5 questions):**
- "What is [your brand]?"
- "Is [your brand] a good choice for [specific application]?"
- "Where can I buy [your product] in China?"

### How to Run the Test

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Open ERNIE (yiyan.baidu.com) and ask each question |
| 2 | Screenshot the response |
| 3 | Record: Does your brand appear? Is it cited? Is it recommended? |
| 4 | Repeat for DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com) |
| 5 | Repeat for Doubao (doubao.com) |
| 6 | Log all results in your tracking spreadsheet |

## Supplementary Monitoring Methods

### Baidu Search Console

Track branded search volume on Baidu. If your GEO efforts are working, more people will search for your brand name over time.

**Key metrics to monitor:**
- Branded search impressions (are more people searching for you?)
- Branded search CTR (are people clicking when they find you?)
- Non-branded search appearances (are you showing up for industry terms?)

### Zhihu Analytics

If you are actively publishing on Zhihu, monitor:
- Answer views (are your answers being read?)
- Upvotes (are readers finding your answers valuable?)
- Follower growth (is your authority growing?)
- Comment engagement (are people discussing your expertise?)

### Media Mention Tracking

Track when your brand appears in Chinese media:
- Set up Google Alerts for your brand name in Chinese
- Use media monitoring tools (Meltwater, Brandwatch) if budget allows
- Manually search Chinese news portals monthly

## Quarterly Deep Audit

Every 3 months, conduct a deeper analysis:

**1. Competitive AI visibility comparison**
Ask the 15 questions but frame them as "best [product category] brands" — see which brands appear and how often.

**2. Content gap analysis**
Identify questions where competitors are cited but you are not. These are your content creation priorities.

**3. Platform coverage review**
Check whether your presence on each platform (Baike, Zhihu, media, website) is up-to-date and consistent.

## What Good GEO Progress Looks Like

| Timeline | Expected Milestone |
|----------|--------------------|
| Month 1–2 | Baidu Baike entry live, Zhihu account established |
| Month 3–4 | First AI mentions appearing (even if incomplete or inaccurate) |
| Month 5–6 | Consistent brand mentions across 1–2 AI models |
| Month 7–9 | Brand cited with data and source references |
| Month 10–12 | Outperforming competitors in AI visibility for core topics |

**Important caveat:** GEO results are not linear. You may see no movement for months, then a sudden jump when a key platform entry gets approved or a media article goes live. Patience and consistency are essential.
