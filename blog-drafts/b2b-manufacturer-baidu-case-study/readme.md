# b2b-manufacturer-baidu-case-study

## EN
Case study of a European industrial automation manufacturer entering the Chinese market through Baidu PPC with BPP. Covers the full 12-month journey: account opening, keyword research, landing page creation, campaign optimization, oCPC implementation, and scaling from zero to 400+ monthly leads at $12 per lead. Based on composite patterns from BPP's B2B client work.
