# geo-get-brand-cited-by-chinese-ai

## EN
Step-by-step guide to getting your brand cited by China's AI models (ERNIE, DeepSeek, Doubao). Covers data sources of each AI model, the 5 essential platforms (Baidu Baike, Zhihu, media, forums, website), GEO content formula, 6-month roadmap, and measurement methods. Specifically addresses how foreign companies without a Chinese entity can establish AI visibility.
