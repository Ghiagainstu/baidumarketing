# How to Get Your Brand Cited by ERNIE, DeepSeek, and Doubao: A Step-by-Step GEO Guide

Your potential customers in China are no longer just searching — they are asking. They ask ERNIE (文心一言) which supplier to trust. They ask DeepSeek which software compares best. They ask Doubao (豆包) what certifications matter in their industry. And these AI models give them answers — complete with brand names, product recommendations, and source citations.

The question is: when Chinese AI models answer questions about your industry, does your brand appear? Or does your competitor's?

This is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — and for international brands targeting China, it is becoming as important as traditional SEO. Here is exactly how to make your brand visible to Chinese AI models, step by step.

## Understanding How Chinese AI Models Find Information

Before optimizing, you need to understand what these models actually cite. Each Chinese AI model has different data sources:

### ERNIE (百度文心一言)

Baidu's flagship AI assistant. Heavily integrated with Baidu's own ecosystem.

**Primary data sources:**
- Baidu Baike (百度百科) — China's Wikipedia equivalent, highest citation weight
- Baidu Zhidao (百度知道) — Q&A platform
- Baidu Wenku (百度文库) — Document library
- Baidu Tieba (百度贴吧) — Forum communities
- Zhihu (知乎) — China's Quora, extremely high citation weight
- Chinese news sites and industry publications
- Your own website (if accessible from China)

**Key insight:** ERNIE prioritizes Baidu's own properties. A Baidu Baike entry carries more weight than a blog post with a million views.

### DeepSeek (深度求索)

China's most talked-about AI model in 2025-2026. Known for reasoning capability.

**Primary data sources:**
- Academic papers and technical publications
- Zhihu (high weight for technical topics)
- GitHub and developer forums
- Chinese industry reports and white papers
- News articles from major Chinese outlets

**Key insight:** DeepSeek favors authoritative, data-heavy content. If your brand appears in Chinese industry reports or academic citations, DeepSeek is more likely to reference you.

### Doubao (字节跳动豆包)

ByteDance's AI assistant. Integrated with Douyin ecosystem.

**Primary data sources:**
- Toutiao (今日头条) news articles
- Douyin video content (transcripts analyzed)
- Chinese web content broadly
- Zhihu
- Baike platforms

**Key insight:** Doubao draws heavily from news and social content. Media coverage in Chinese outlets directly impacts your visibility here.

## The 5 Platforms You Must Be On

To be cited by Chinese AI models, you need presence on these five platforms. Here is how to establish each one — including what to do if you do not have a Chinese entity.

### 1. Baidu Baike (百度百科) — Highest Priority

Baidu Baike is the single most important platform for GEO in China. It is the equivalent of having a Wikipedia entry — but with more direct impact on AI citations.

**Why it matters:**
- ERNIE pulls directly from Baidu Baike for company and product queries
- A verified Baike entry signals legitimacy to all Chinese AI models
- Chinese buyers check Baidu Baike before making B2B purchasing decisions

**How to create a Baidu Baike entry as a foreign company:**

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Register a Baidu account (requires Chinese phone number or Baidu international account) | Day 1 |
| 2 | Gather source materials: company registration, official website, press mentions, certifications | Day 1–3 |
| 3 | Draft the Baike entry in Chinese: company overview, history, products, certifications, global presence | Day 3–5 |
| 4 | Submit for review with citations from at least 3 independent Chinese sources | Day 5 |
| 5 | Baidu review process (1–2 rounds of revision expected) | 5–15 business days |

**Common rejection reasons:**
- Insufficient third-party citations (Baidu requires independent sources, not your own website)
- Promotional language (Baike entries must be neutral and factual)
- Machine-translated content (human-written Chinese is required)
- Missing key information (registration details, notable achievements)

### 2. Zhihu (知乎) — High Priority

Zhihu is China's largest professional Q&A platform, similar to Quora. Chinese AI models cite Zhihu extensively because its content is structured as questions and answers — perfect for AI extraction.

**Strategy for foreign brands:**

**Step 1: Create an official Zhihu account**
- Requires Chinese phone number for registration
- Alternatively, create a personal account for a company representative
- Complete the profile with company information and expertise areas

**Step 2: Identify relevant questions**
- Search for questions about your industry, product category, or market
- Focus on questions with high follower counts (100+ followers = high visibility)
- Look for questions where existing answers lack depth or accuracy

**Step 3: Write authoritative answers**
- Answer format that performs best for AI citation:
  - Start with a direct answer (1–2 sentences)
  - Provide structured data (comparison tables, specifications, benchmarks)
  - Include real examples and case studies
  - End with your professional credentials
- Minimum 800 characters for substantive answers
- Include images, charts, or tables when possible

**Step 4: Build answer authority**
- Answers with more upvotes get cited more frequently by AI
- Engage with comments on your answers
- Cross-reference your answers with other platforms

**Important:** Zhihu's community actively penalizes overt advertising. Your answers must be genuinely helpful. Mention your brand naturally within expert advice — never as the primary purpose of the answer.

### 3. Chinese Media Coverage — Medium-High Priority

AI models cite news articles. When your brand is mentioned in Chinese media, it becomes part of the training data and citation pool.

**How to get Chinese media coverage as a foreign brand:**

- **Industry publications:** Contribute expert articles to Chinese trade publications in your sector
- **Press releases:** Issue Chinese-language press releases through distribution services (PR Newswire China, Business Wire China)
- **Interviews:** Offer executive interviews to Chinese journalists covering your industry
- **Awards and recognition:** Submit for Chinese industry awards — award listings are frequently cited

**Cost range:** ¥5,000–50,000 ($690–$6,900) per placement depending on publication tier.

### 4. Industry Forums and Communities — Medium Priority

Baidu Tieba (贴吧), industry-specific forums, and WeChat public accounts all feed into AI training data.

**Action items:**
- Register on the most active Baidu Tieba forum for your industry
- Participate in discussions with genuinely helpful contributions
- Create a WeChat Official Account (requires Chinese entity or partner) and publish industry insights
- Engage in relevant WeChat group discussions (content from some groups is indexed)

### 5. Your Own Website — Foundation Layer

Your website must be accessible from China and contain structured, factual content.

**Requirements:**
- Chinese-language content (not just translated — localized for Chinese readers)
- Accessible from mainland China (test with a Chinese server or VPN)
- Fast loading (under 3 seconds from Chinese servers)
- Structured data markup where applicable
- Factual, comprehensive content (product specs, certifications, case studies)

## The GEO Content Formula

Not all content gets cited equally. Here is what Chinese AI models prefer to quote:

### Content That Gets Cited

| Content Type | Citation Likelihood | Example |
|-------------|-------------------|---------|
| Comparison tables | Very high | "Brand A vs Brand B: 10 Specifications Compared" |
| Data and statistics | Very high | "2026 China Market Report: 500 Manufacturers Surveyed" |
| Step-by-step guides | High | "How to Choose [Product]: 5-Step Decision Framework" |
| FAQ sections | High | "Top 10 Questions About [Your Industry]" |
| Case studies with numbers | High | "How Company X Reduced Costs by 40% with [Product]" |
| Expert opinions with credentials | Medium-high | "According to [Name], [Title] at [Company]..." |
| General company descriptions | Low | "Our company was founded in..." |
| Marketing copy | Very low | "Best-in-class solutions for..." |

### The Golden Rule

**Be the most helpful, data-rich, factual source on your topic in Chinese.** If your content answers a question more completely and more accurately than anything else available, AI models will find it and cite it.

## Measuring GEO Success

Unlike PPC, GEO does not have built-in analytics. Here is how to measure it:

### Manual Testing (Monthly)

Ask each Chinese AI model the same set of 10–15 questions about your industry and record:
- Does your brand appear in the answer?
- Is your brand cited as a source?
- How does your visibility compare to competitors?

### Tools for Monitoring

- **Manual queries:** Ask ERNIE, DeepSeek, and Doubao directly — log results in a spreadsheet
- **Baidu search monitoring:** Track branded search volume on Baidu (increasing volume = growing awareness)
- **Zhihu analytics:** Monitor answer views, upvotes, and follower growth
- **Media monitoring:** Track Chinese media mentions using tools like Meltwater or Brandwatch (China coverage)
