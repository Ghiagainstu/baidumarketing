# How to Open a Baidu PPC Account as a Foreign Company: Step-by-Step Guide

You have decided to advertise on Baidu. You know the audience is there — 600 million monthly active users, the dominant search engine in China, and a platform where CPCs are still lower than Google in most B2B verticals. But the moment you try to open an account, you hit a wall: the registration page is in Chinese, the verification requires documents you do not have, and the support hotline only speaks Mandarin.

This is the single biggest reason international brands delay Baidu advertising. Not budget. Not strategy. The account opening process itself.

Here is exactly what the process looks like, where the bottlenecks are, and how to get through them without flying to Beijing.

## Two Paths to a Baidu PPC Account

There are two ways to open a Baidu advertising account:

### Path 1: Direct Registration (Chinese Entity Required)

If your company has a Chinese business license (营业执照), you can register directly on Baidu's marketing platform at `e.baidu.com`. The process is straightforward:

1. Visit `e.baidu.com` and click "注册" (Register)
2. Enter your Chinese mobile phone number
3. Submit your business license number
4. Complete V-verification (V 标认证) — Baidu's identity verification system
5. Set up billing and deposit funds

The minimum top-up is ¥5,000 (approximately $690 USD). Baidu uses a prepaid model — your ads run from this balance, and you top up when it runs low.

**The problem:** Most international companies do not have a Chinese business license. This path is only available to companies registered in mainland China.

### Path 2: Authorized Partner Route (No Chinese Entity Needed)

International brands can advertise on Baidu without a Chinese business license by working with an authorized Baidu partner. This is the standard path for overseas companies.

An authorized partner like BPP handles:
- Account registration under the partner's verified Chinese entity
- V-verification and compliance review
- Fund deposits and billing setup
- Ongoing account management and optimization

You still get full visibility into your account — campaign data, spend reports, keyword performance — but the partner holds the account registration credentials.

## What Documents Do You Need?

Even through a partner, Baidu requires certain documents for compliance. Here is what you will need to prepare:

### Required for All Advertisers

| Document | Details | Format |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Business registration certificate | Your company's official registration from your home country | Scanned copy, color |
| Authorized signatory letter | A letter authorizing the partner to open an account on your behalf | On company letterhead |
| Advertiser information form | Company name, contact person, industry, website URL | Filled via partner |

### Industry-Specific Requirements

Some industries require additional licensing:

| Industry | Additional License Required |
|----------|-----------------------------|
| Healthcare / Medical devices | Medical device advertising license (医疗器械广告审查证明) |
| Financial services | Financial business license from relevant authority |
| Education | School operating permit or education training license |
| Food & supplements | Food business license (食品经营许可证) |
| Franchise / MLM | Franchise registration certificate |

If your industry falls into a restricted category, Baidu's compliance team reviews on a case-by-case basis. Working with an experienced partner matters here — they know which documentation patterns get approved and which get rejected.

## The Verification Process: What to Expect

Once documents are submitted, here is the typical timeline:

| Stage | Timeline | What Happens |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Document submission | Day 1 | Partner submits all documents to Baidu |
| Initial review | 1–3 business days | Baidu checks document completeness and accuracy |
| V-verification | 2–5 business days | Baidu verifies the entity and advertiser information |
| Account activation | 1 business day | Account goes live, billing is set up |
| First deposit | Same day as activation | Minimum ¥5,000 (~$690 USD) |

**Total time from submission to live account: 3–10 business days.**

The most common delays are:
- Document quality issues (blurry scans, missing pages)
- Industry-specific license gaps
- Chinese translation inconsistencies between documents

## Common Reasons Accounts Get Rejected

Understanding rejection reasons upfront saves weeks of back-and-forth:

**1. Incomplete business registration**
Your home country registration must be current (not expired) and include the official stamp or seal.

**2. Website compliance issues**
Your landing page must be accessible from China, load within 3 seconds on Chinese networks, and comply with Chinese advertising law. Machine-translated landing pages are frequently flagged.

**3. Prohibited content**
Baidu prohibits advertising for: gambling, lottery, illegal goods/services, certain pharmaceutical products, and weapons. This list is stricter than Google's.

**4. Restricted industry without license**
If your industry requires a specific license (healthcare, finance, education) and you cannot provide one, the account will be rejected.

### Billing Models

Baidu offers several billing models. For most new advertisers, CPC (Cost Per Click) is the starting point:

- **CPC**: You pay when someone clicks your ad. Standard model for search ads.
- **oCPC**: AI-optimized CPC. Baidu's system adjusts bids automatically based on conversion probability. Recommended after you accumulate 30+ conversions.
- **CPM**: Cost per 1,000 impressions. Used primarily for brand awareness and feed ads.
