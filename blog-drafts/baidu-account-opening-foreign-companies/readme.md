# baidu-account-opening-foreign-companies

## EN
Step-by-step guide to opening a Baidu PPC account from outside China. Covers two registration paths (direct vs authorized partner), required documents, verification timelines, common rejection reasons, and budget requirements. Explains how BPP handles the entire account opening process for international brands.
