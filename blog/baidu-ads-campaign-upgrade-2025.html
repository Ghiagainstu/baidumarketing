Baidu Ads Campaign Upgrades 2025: What Actually Changed

Oct 16, 2025 8 min read Platform By Baidu PPC Pro Team

If you are managing Baidu campaigns from outside China, you already know the platform has its own logic. Interface patterns, naming conventions, and optimization workflows are nothing like Google Ads or Meta Ads Manager. So when Baidu ships an update to its campaign building interface, it is worth knowing exactly what changed — and whether it affects your workflow.

This article breaks down three real platform upgrades from Baidu's October 2025 product update, explains what they do in plain terms, and flags which ones matter for international advertisers.

💡
BPP Angle:

These upgrades are genuinely useful — but they only matter if you have a live Baidu account. The biggest barrier for overseas companies is still account setup. BPP handles that part for you.

🚀 Upgrade 1: Simplified Unit-Level Campaign Creation

What changed:

Baidu redesigned the unit creation workflow to reduce steps and consolidate options. The old flow used a side panel for selecting which campaign your new unit belonged to. That panel is now a standalone page — cleaner, easier to navigate, and with better visibility into campaign status.

Key improvements:

  • Filter by campaign attribute and status — find the right campaign faster when creating a new unit
  • Filter by marketing goal and bidding method — narrows your options to what you actually need
  • Creative recommendations built into the flow — the system now prompts you to use high-performing ad templates before you finish
  • Landing page setup simplified — removed the URL tracking suffix field; if you need tracking parameters, add them directly to the URL
  • Navigation bar streamlined — shows the full campaign hierarchy so you always know where your unit sits

What this means for you:

Fewer clicks, less guesswork, and better visibility into your campaign structure. If you are managing multiple campaigns with dozens of units, the cleaner navigation alone is worth it.

⚠️ Scope: This upgrade covers Lead Generation, Local Store, Website Links, and E-commerce store goals. App Promotion, Product Catalog, and Baijiahao goals are planned for later.

🤖 Upgrade 2: AI Copywriting Tool Upgraded

What changed:

The "Generate Copy" tool inside the ad creation flow now has significantly more intelligence. When you trigger it, the system automatically pulls your landing page content, extracts your business keywords and selling points, and pre-fills that information before generating anything.

How it works:

  1. You enter the unit creation or ad creation flow
  2. Click "Generate Copy" — the right-side panel opens automatically
  3. The system reads your landing page and fills in business category, selling points, and keywords
  4. You review, click "Start Generating," and the system produces copy based on industry knowledge and high-CTR creative patterns from Baidu's platform
  5. You can edit any generated line — add a wildcard, trim text, or delete — before confirming

The wildcard rule changed too:

Starting with this update, you can only add one keyword wildcard per ad title (plus one location wildcard if applicable). If you had two keyword wildcards in existing ads, those will continue to show as before. But if you edit an existing ad, the system will enforce the one-wildcard limit before saving.

⚠️ Wildcard Alert: If you currently use two keyword wildcards in a single title, Baidu will now warn you before saving. Existing ads are grandfathered in, but editing them triggers the new limit.

What this means for you:

AI-generated copy that matches your actual landing page content — not generic templates. The system does the heavy lifting on first drafts. You still review and approve, but the starting point is much better than blank fields.

🔍 Upgrade 3: Campaign Structure and Targeting Filters

What changed:

The campaign building interface now gives you more control over targeting and structure before you even write an ad:

  • Filter campaigns by marketing goal and bidding method in one view
  • See campaign status (active, paused, etc.) at a glance
  • Select your target campaign and create new units with full context visible

This ties directly to the UI improvements in Upgrade 1 — the goal is end-to-end efficiency from campaign selection to unit creation to ad writing.

🔒 What Does Not Change

Baidu has explicitly stated: these upgrades only change the page experience. Product functionality is unchanged.

Your existing campaigns, bids, keywords, and settings are unaffected. This is a UI/workflow improvement, not a policy or algorithm change.
