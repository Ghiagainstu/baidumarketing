China's internet population hit 1.125 billion in late 2025, with an 80.1% penetration rate. That number alone doesn't tell you much. What matters for advertisers: 99.6% of those users are on mobile, they spend an average of 32.5 hours online per week, and 90.3% use mobile payment.

The CNNIC 57th Statistical Report (published February 2026) puts real data behind the "huge market" pitch. Here are the figures worth building campaigns around.

The Mobile-First Reality

Mobile isn't just dominant in China — it's effectively the only channel that matters. 99.6% of internet users access the web via mobile phone. That means:

  • Your landing pages must be optimized for mobile-first design
  • Your ad creatives need to work at small screen sizes
  • Your conversion flows need to be frictionless on touch interfaces
  • Hosting in China or via CDN is essential for fast mobile load times

Time Spent Online: 32.5 Hours Per Week

That's roughly 4.5 hours per day. Chinese internet users are spending more time online than ever before. But here's the nuance: that time is fragmented across multiple platforms rather than concentrated on any single one.

The implication for advertisers: you need to be present across multiple touchpoints, not just Baidu search. A comprehensive China strategy includes Baidu search for high-intent capture, Baidu feed for discovery and awareness, and potentially Douyin or Xiaohongshu for certain audiences.

Mobile Payment: 90.3% Adoption

Mobile payment penetration at 90.3% means the path from discovery to purchase is shorter than almost anywhere else in the world. When someone sees your ad on Baidu, decides they want your product, and has WeChat Pay or Alipay already set up — the conversion barrier is minimal.

For e-commerce advertisers, this is crucial. For B2B service providers, it means your lead capture forms should integrate with WeChat for instant follow-up capability.

What This Means for Your Budget

If you're allocating spend to China, these numbers suggest a few things:

Mobile-first creative is non-negotiable. With 99.6% mobile access, desktop-optimized campaigns are leaving money on the table.

Cross-platform presence compounds. The average user isn't just on Baidu — they're also on Douyin, WeChat, Xiaohongshu. Your brand needs to be discoverable across these touchpoints.

The addressable market is massive but fragmented. 1.125 billion users sounds like everyone, but they're distributed across dozens of platforms and content types. Niche targeting on the right platforms beats mass reach on Baidu alone.

The data makes the case for digital-first spend. The question is whether your current structure can execute across the fragmented Chinese digital landscape — and that's where a partner with deep platform knowledge becomes essential.
