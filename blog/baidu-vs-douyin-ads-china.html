Baidu vs Douyin Ads: Which Works Better for B2B in China?

2026-06-23 8 min strategy By Baidu PPC Pro Team

If you are planning to advertise in China, you have probably heard two names over and over: Baidu and Douyin. Baidu is China's dominant search engine. Douyin (the Chinese version of TikTok) is the country's most engaging short-video platform. Both offer advertising. Both reach hundreds of millions of users. But for B2B advertisers, they are fundamentally different tools.

Choosing the wrong platform does not just waste budget — it wastes months of effort while your competitors move ahead. Here is a clear, honest comparison to help you decide.

The Core Difference: Intent vs. Discovery

The single most important distinction:

Baidu = Search intent. Users are actively looking for something. They type a question or a product name into a search bar. Your ad appears when they are already interested.

Douyin = Discovery. Users are passively watching videos. Your ad interrupts their feed. You have 3–5 seconds to capture attention before they swipe away.

This difference shapes everything: cost structure, conversion quality, content requirements, and which business types benefit most.

For B2B advertisers, Baidu's audience is inherently more valuable because users are already in a research or purchase mindset. Douyin's audience is larger but mostly in entertainment mode.

Douyin looks cheaper at first glance — lower CPCs and CPMs. But the cost per lead tells the real story. Because Douyin users are not actively searching for solutions, the conversion rate from click to lead is 3–5x lower than Baidu for B2B campaigns. The result: Douyin CPL is typically 2–3x higher than Baidu for the same B2B lead quality.

Baidu leads are further along in the buying journey. They have already identified a need, searched for solutions, and clicked on your ad because it matched what they were looking for. Douyin leads are often "just curious" — they saw an interesting video and filled out a form without deep purchase intent.

Content Requirements

The type of content you need is very different on each platform:

Baidu

  • Search ads: Text-based ad copy with strong Chinese keyword targeting
  • Feed ads: Image + text combinations, native article style
  • Landing pages: Detailed Chinese landing pages with product specs, certifications, lead forms
  • Content production cost: Low to medium — text-heavy, professional tone

Douyin

  • Video ads: 15–60 second vertical videos, entertainment-quality production
  • Live commerce: Real-time product demonstrations with host interaction
  • KOL (influencer) partnerships: Industry-specific creators promoting your product
  • Content production cost: High — requires video production, editing, and ongoing content creation

For B2B companies entering China, Baidu's content requirements are significantly easier to meet. A well-written Chinese landing page can drive results for months. Douyin requires constant new video content — and B2B product videos are inherently harder to make engaging than consumer content.

When to Choose Baidu

Baidu is the better choice when:

  • You sell high-value B2B products (manufacturing components, software, professional services)
  • Your buyers search before purchasing (procurement teams, engineers, IT managers)
  • You need leads, not just awareness (form submissions, phone calls, demo requests)
  • You are entering China for the first time (search captures existing demand without needing brand awareness)
  • Your budget is limited (more efficient CPL, less content production cost)

When to Choose Douyin

Douyin makes sense when:

  • You sell visually appealing B2C products (fashion, food, cosmetics, electronics)
  • Brand awareness is the primary goal (you need Chinese consumers to know your brand exists)
  • You have video production resources (in-house or agency capability for ongoing content)
  • Your product demos well on video (machines in action, before/after results, live demonstrations)
  • You are already established in China and want to add a demand generation layer on top of search

The Best Strategy: Baidu First, Douyin Second

For most B2B companies entering China, the optimal sequence is:

  1. Start with Baidu search ads to capture existing demand and generate leads from day one
  2. Use Baidu feed ads to extend reach to users researching related topics
  3. After 6–12 months of data, evaluate whether Douyin adds incremental value
  4. If adding Douyin, use it as an awareness and retargeting layer — not as the primary lead source

This sequence works because Baidu provides immediate, measurable ROI while Douyin requires brand awareness investment that pays off over longer time horizons.

Platform-Specific Challenges for International Advertisers

Baidu Challenges

  • Account opening requires a Chinese entity or authorized partner
  • Ad copy and landing pages must be in Chinese
  • Compliance review is stricter than Google
  • Requires Chinese keyword research (cannot reuse English campaigns)

Douyin Challenges

  • Video content must be produced in Chinese with Chinese talent
  • Cultural nuance is critical — what works on TikTok does not work on Douyin
  • Account verification for foreign brands is complex
  • Live commerce requires Chinese-speaking hosts
  • Algorithm favors accounts that post daily — requires sustained content investment
