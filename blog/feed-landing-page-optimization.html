Landing Page

Optimizing Your Feed Ad Landing Pages for More Conversions

November 20244 min read

A landing page bridges the gap between ad creative and your product or service. For Baidu feed campaigns, small tweaks regularly lift conversion rates by 15-30%.

15-30%
Conversion lift from landing page optimization

The Big Wins

Load Speed Under 3 Seconds

Feed users scroll fast. If your page doesn't load instantly, they're gone. Host in China or use a CDN with China edge nodes.

Match the Creative's Promise

If your ad shows a "Free Quote," the landing page should prominently feature a quote form. Continuity = trust.

Single Clear Action

One primary CTA — fill the form, call now, download the brochure. More options = fewer conversions.

Trust Signals Front and Center

Client logos, certifications, years in business, industry awards. Feed users are in discovery mode — they need convincing.

Form Optimization

The biggest lever for most B2B landing pages:

  • Limit to 3-4 fields. Name, company, email, phone. That's it. Every additional field drops completion rate.
  • Mobile-friendly form design. Large input fields, proper keyboard types (number pad for phone), easy tap targets.
  • Inline validation. Tell users immediately if their email format is wrong, not after they hit submit.

Testing Priorities

If you can only test one thing, test the headline. It's the first thing users see and has the biggest impact on perceived relevance.

After headline, test in this order:

  1. CTA button text and color
  2. Form field count and order
  3. Trust signal placement
  4. Page layout
