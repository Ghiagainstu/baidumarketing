Baidu EEAT 6.0 and the New GEO Reality

Jul 10, 2026 7 min read Strategy By Baidu PPC Pro Team

Two things happened in July 2026 that change the rules for every international brand publishing content in China: the Cyberspace Administration launched its second "Clean AI Application Chaos" campaign, and Baidu simultaneously rolled out EEAT 6.0 — linking SEO, GEO, and SEM compliance into a single enforcement system.

🗺️ What Is GEO and Why Now?

Generative Engine Optimization optimizes brand content so AI models — Wenxin, Douyin Doubao, DeepSeek, Kimi — cite it in answers. AI search penetration hit 38.7% in Q2 2026. The GEO market projects ¥10B+ with 65% CAGR.

Traditional SEOGEO
TargetRank on results pagesBe cited in AI answers
MetricKeyword rank, UV trafficAI citation rate, brand mentions
LogicKeyword matching + backlinksVector recall + authority scoring
ContentSingle-page, keyword-drivenMulti-source, cross-platform matrix

SEO asks: "Where does my page rank?" GEO asks: "Does the AI mention my brand — or my competitor's?"

🔒 What Baidu EEAT 6.0 Enforces

CROSS-DOMAIN LINKAGE. One violation triggers penalties across all three domains simultaneously:

ViolationSEOGEOSEM
AI corpus poisoningDomain de-rankingAI source delistingAccount suspension
Fake source implantationEntity risk-listCitation blacklistPaid account ban
Low-quality floodingTrust score collapseZero AI citationBudget freeze

This is structural. Previously, paid search and organic content operated in separate silos. Under EEAT 6.0, they share a unified trust score — and the enforcement is retroactive.

🛡️ Government Crackdown: Four Types of AI Poisoning

  • Bulk auto-generated flooding — mass AI text distorting industry semantics
  • Fake credential fabrication — phantom certifications, made-up media endorsements
  • Hidden vector tampering — invisible code tricking AI crawlers
  • Competitor poisoning — feeding negative data about rivals

Industry testing: ~68% of outsourced SEO agencies have engaged in at least one form. The risk is concentrated on advertisers who outsourced content without verifying methods.

📋 What International Brands Must Do Now

🎯 Immediate Action Checklist

  • Audit existing content: verify no fake endorsements, automated flooding, or hidden SEO code
  • Reframe for GEO: structured knowledge bases, not keyword-stuffed pages
  • Build verifiable authority: official brand entities on Baidu Baike, real author credentials, cited sources
  • Monitor AI visibility: track citation rates across Wenxin, Doubao, DeepSeek, Kimi
  • Cut gray-hat vendors: any outsourced "SEO package" is now a compliance liability
