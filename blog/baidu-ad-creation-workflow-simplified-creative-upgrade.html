Baidu rolled out a trio of upgrades to its ad creation platform on October 16, 2025. They target three pain points every search advertiser knows: slow campaign setup, blank-page syndrome when writing ad copy, and headline formatting rules that cause unnecessary rejections.

Here is what changed — and how overseas advertisers benefit.

🚀 Upgrade 1: Simplified Unit Creation Workflow

Setting up ad units (ad groups) in Baidu used to mean navigating multiple nested screens, re-specifying landing pages, and guessing which plan to attach a new unit to.

The new workflow:

💡 What Improved

✅ Filter plans by marketing objective and bidding method before creating a unit
✅ New dedicated page (not side panel) — cleaner view, shows plan status
✅ Creative recommendations auto-suggested during unit creation
✅ Advanced styles accessible via shortcut — no need to navigate separately
✅ Landing page setup simplified — tracking URL suffixes removed from required fields

Bottom line: Fewer clicks, less room for error, faster launch.

🚀 Upgrade 2: Copy Generation Tool Gets Smarter

The "Generate Copy" tool within Baidu's creative editor now does two things it did not before:

1. Auto-fills context: The system reads your landing page and campaign settings, and pre-fills your business name, key selling points, and target keywords. You confirm these inputs, click "Generate," and get drafts.

2. Better generation quality: The AI now draws on industry-specific knowledge and historical high-CTR creative data to generate copy. This is not generic template output — it reflects what has actually performed in your category.

🎯 How to Use It

  • Enter unit or creative creation → click "Generate Copy"
  • System auto-fills business info from landing page → confirm or adjust
  • Click "Start Generate" → review drafts
  • Hover to edit (add wildcards, trim text) → click "Confirm" to insert

🚀 Upgrade 3: Title Wildcard Limit — One Keyword Wildcard Max

This is the most impactful change for ad structure:

Before: You could insert up to 2 keyword wildcards in a single headline. Result: repetitive headlines like `Buy [Keyword] — Best [Keyword] Supplier`. After: Maximum 1 keyword wildcard per headline. You can still add a separate geographic wildcard (e.g., `[City]`).

Rules:

  • New creatives: 1 keyword wildcard max. System prevents saving headlines with 2+.
  • Existing creatives with 2 wildcards: Continue to display as before, but editing them now enforces the 1-wildcard limit.

This forces cleaner headlines. No more keyword-stuffed titles.
