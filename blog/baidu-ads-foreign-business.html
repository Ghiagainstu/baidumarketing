Why Baidu Works Better Than Social Platforms

Social media advertising (Douyin, WeChat, Xiaohongshu) is great for awareness and discovery. But when someone is ready to buy, they go to Baidu. The difference:

  • Social = interest-based targeting. You show ads to people who might be interested based on demographics and behavior.
  • Search = intent-based targeting. You show ads to people who are actively looking for your product category right now.

For lead generation and direct response, intent wins. The conversion rate on Baidu search ads typically exceeds social platforms because you're reaching people at the moment of decision.
