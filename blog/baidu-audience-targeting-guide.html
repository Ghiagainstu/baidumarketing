Baidu Audience Targeting: A Complete Guide for Advertisers

Oct 15, 2019 7 min read By Baidu PPC Pro Team

If you have run Baidu PPC for any length of time, you have likely felt the frustration: you bid on the right keywords, your ads show, but the traffic does not convert. Irrelevant users click, your budget drains, and your CPA climbs.

The problem is not your keywords. It is that keywords alone do not know who is searching. Two people can search the exact same term with completely different intent. Baidu Audience Targeting solves this by layering user profile data on top of your keyword targeting, so your ads reach the right people — not just the right queries.

What Is Baidu Audience Targeting?

Baidu Audience Targeting uses Baidu's massive user database to find your ideal audience based on who they are, what they are interested in, what they have searched for, and what apps they use. When you combine audience targeting with your keyword bids, the system prioritizes showing your ads to users who match your target profile.

Think of it as adding a filter between your keywords and your ad delivery: keywords determine the search queries you appear for, audience targeting determines which of those searchers actually see your ad.

Four Targeting Dimensions

Baidu offers four main ways to define your audience. Each one works differently, and the most effective campaigns combine multiple dimensions.

1. Feature Targeting (Demographics)

This is the most straightforward layer. You select users based on who they are:

  • Gender — male or female
  • Age — by age range
  • Interest — Baidu categorizes users into 25+ interest segments based on their browsing and search behavior
💡
Interest categories include: Financial Management, Real Estate, Travel, Automotive, Education, Health, Beauty, Fashion, Gaming, Food & Drink, Business Services, Home Appliances, and more. If your product fits a specific interest bucket, use it as your primary targeting filter.

2. Behavior Targeting (Search & Browse History)

This is where audience targeting gets powerful. You define your audience based on what they have actually done:

  • Search history — target users who have searched for specific keywords (up to 1,000 keywords). If someone searched for "industrial pump supplier" last week, they are a warm lead for your B2B campaign.
  • Browsing history — target users who have visited specific URLs (up to 1,000 URLs). If someone visited your competitor's pricing page, they are in-market for your product.
✅
Pro tip: Search history targeting is one of Baidu's most underused features. Most advertisers rely on keyword matching alone, but targeting users who already searched for related terms gives you an intent advantage that keyword-only bidding cannot match.

3. App Targeting (Mobile Only)

For mobile campaigns, you can target users by the types of apps they have installed. You cannot target specific apps, but you can target app categories:

Office & Daily Apps

  • Efficiency office
  • Education & training
  • Travel & navigation
  • Financial management
  • Online shopping
  • Beauty care
  • Food & catering
  • Medical health
  • Social communication
  • News & reading

Game Categories

  • Casual puzzle
  • Sports competition
  • Action shooting
  • Chess & board games
  • Role-playing (RPG)
  • Strategy games
  • Racing
  • Nutrition & fitness
  • Cosplay & dress-up
  • Other games

If your product targets mobile gamers, for example, you can layer game category app targeting on top of your interest and behavior filters to build a precise audience that keyword targeting alone would never reach.

4. ID Targeting (Precision Retargeting)

This is the most accurate targeting method Baidu offers, but it requires you to already have user data. You upload known identifiers and Baidu matches them against its user database:

ID Type Description Best For
Baidu ID Baidu account ID (logged-in users) Retargeting existing customers
IMEI Android device ID Mobile app retargeting
IDFA iOS device ID iOS app retargeting
Cookie ID Browser cookie Web retargeting
Phone Number Mobile number (hashed) CRM-based targeting
⚠️
Important: ID targeting requires you to already own this data. If you have not collected user IDs or phone numbers from your existing customers, start with feature and behavior targeting first, then build toward ID targeting as your data grows.

📋 Key Takeaways

  • Baidu Audience Targeting adds a user-profile filter on top of keyword targeting — reaches the right people, not just the right queries
  • Four dimensions: feature (demographics), behavior (search/browse history), app (mobile categories), ID (precision retargeting)
  • Search history and browsing history targeting are the most underused — and most effective — tools for improving traffic quality
  • Combine multiple dimensions for best results: interest + search history + app category is a powerful stack
  • ID targeting is the most accurate but requires existing customer data
