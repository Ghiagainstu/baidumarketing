What Is the Baidu V Sign and Why Your Account Needs It

Jun 11, 2019 5 min read Platform Updates By Baidu PPC Pro Team

If you look at Baidu search results closely — both organic listings and paid ads — you will notice a small "v" symbol next to some results. That is the Baidu V sign, and for foreign advertisers, it matters more than most people realize.

The V sign is Baidu's way of telling users: "this business is verified." Baidu has checked the business registration documents, confirmed the entity is legitimate, and placed a stamp of trust on the result. It is a simple icon, but in a market where trust drives buying decisions, it carries real weight.

And here is the part that catches some advertisers by surprise: you have to get the V sign to run Baidu PPC ads. It is not optional. It is a requirement.

"The V sign is not a badge you earn — it is the price of entry. Without it, you cannot run paid ads on Baidu. And each year, you need to renew it or your account gets paused."

— Baidu PPC Pro Team

What the V Sign Actually Does

Baidu's V sign appears right after the display URL and the date on both organic and paid search results. Its purpose is straightforward: it tells users that Baidu has verified the authenticity of your business registration. When someone in China sees the V sign, they know the result is from a real, registered company — not a fly-by-night operator.

That matters because Chinese consumers are cautious. Fake listings and counterfeit sellers exist, and users have learned to look for signals of legitimacy. The V sign gives them that signal instantly.

The Three V Levels

Baidu uses a tiered V sign system. The level shown next to your result depends on how long you have been verified:

Level Verification Duration What It Signals
V1 Less than 1 year Newly verified by Baidu
V2 1 to 2 years Established track record
V3 3+ years (top class) Highest trust level

The logic is simple: the longer you have been verified, the higher your V level. V3 is the top class. It tells users your business has passed Baidu's scrutiny for three years or more. For a foreign brand entering China, moving from V1 to V3 becomes a visible sign of your commitment to the market.

Why the V Sign Matters for Your Business

Beyond the fact that it is required for PPC, the V sign brings three practical benefits:

  • Higher click-through rates — Users trust verified results. When someone sees the V sign next to your listing, they are more likely to click. And when they click on a verified result, they are more likely to convert, because the trust has already been established.
  • Brand authority — A V3 sign next to your ad says you have been operating in China for years. For a new foreign brand, building that perception from day one is hard. The V sign helps close the trust gap.
  • Counterfeit protection — With the V sign, users can easily tell which result is the real you. Counterfeiters and unauthorized resellers lose their advantage because their listings do not carry the verified badge.
💡
Why It Works

The V sign addresses a specific problem in the Chinese market: consumers have been burned by fake products and scam listings. A Baidu-verified badge signals that a real company stands behind the result. For foreign brands without local recognition, this is one of the fastest ways to earn initial trust.

Annual Renewal: What You Need to Know

The V sign is not a one-time setup. Baidu requires annual verification. The process differs depending on your business registration type:

Registrations with an Expiration Date

Some business registrations — like Hong Kong business registrations — have a limited validity period, typically one year. If yours falls into this category, Baidu requires an authentication approval process before the expiration date of your registration. The documents needed are the same as what you submitted when opening your account, plus an updated business registration certificate.

The critical deadline: submit the new documents at least 30 days before the expiration date. When Baidu processes the renewal of your Business Registration Certificate, it triggers a special approval process. If you miss the deadline, your account will be rejected, and all account activities will be paused on the expiry date. Once the authentication is approved, the account resumes normal operation. The process typically takes 3 to 5 business days, though in practice it often completes in 1 to 3 days.

⚠️
Critical

Your account cannot have a zero balance when the renewal process runs. Make sure there is sufficient funds before the authentication deadline hits. A zero balance will block the process entirely.

Registrations with No Expiration Date

For business registrations that never expire, Baidu still requires annual censorship of your account. The difference: you do not need to submit any documents. You simply ask Baidu to start the annual review process. Your account will be paused on the anniversary date until the review passes, then it resumes. The same 3 to 5 business day timeline applies.

✅
From Experience

The annual renewal process often completes faster than the stated timeline. We have seen most renewals go through in 1 to 3 business days instead of the standard 5. But we always recommend starting 30 days early to account for any document issues.

The Cost

The V sign verification costs CNY 600 per year. At roughly $85 USD, it is a small price for the trust and compliance it provides. Compare that to the cost of having your account paused for missing the deadline — the annual fee looks like a bargain.

Key Takeaway

The Baidu V sign is more than a trust badge — it is mandatory for PPC advertisers and has real business impact:

  • You need the V sign to run Baidu PPC ads — it is not optional
  • Three levels (V1 → V2 → V3) signal your tenure as a verified business
  • Annual renewal costs ¥600 and requires proactive document submission
  • Start the renewal process 30 days before expiration to avoid account suspension
  • Accounts with no expiration still need annual review — no new documents needed
