2020 Baidu B2B Industry Insights: What China's Industrial Search Data Reveals

Jul 9, 2020 7 min By Baidu PPC Pro Team
# 2020 Baidu B2B Industry Insights: What China's Industrial Search Data Reveals > Baidu processed millions of B2B industrial search queries in 2020. The patterns reveal exactly how Chinese business buyers research products, which industries lead demand, and where the platform competition stands. Here is what the data says — and what it means for overseas companies. ## The Big Picture: B2B Search Demand Bounces Back Strong The first thing that stands out in Baidu's 2020 B2B Industry Report: after the early-2020 downturn, B2B search demand on Baidu recovered rapidly. By April 2020, year-over-year B2B search volume was already outpacing 2019 levels. Businesses did not stop buying — they moved their supplier research online faster than anyone expected.

The split between mobile and PC tells its own story: about 70% of B2B search traffic comes from mobile devices, showing that Chinese business buyers do their supplier research on the go — on factory floors, construction sites, and during field visits. But 30% still searches from desktop, which means you need campaigns on both channels to capture the full market. ## China's Top 10 B2B Segments: Where the Demand Is Baidu's search data identified ten dominant B2B industry categories. The remaining 30% can cover niche terms. This targeting approach typically produces 2-3x better ROAS than broad-category bidding.
## How Chinese B2B Buyers Actually Search and Decide Understanding search behavior is the difference between running ads and running profitable ads. Baidu's report maps the exact journey Chinese B2B buyers take from first search to final purchase. ### The Search Journey: Three Stages **Stage 1 — Awareness**: Buyers start with generic B2B terms. They are casting a wide net. At this stage they click on Baidu Baike entries, Baidu Zhidao Q&A, and industry content on Baijiahao. They are educating themselves — not yet ready to contact a supplier. **Stage 2 — Comparison**: Searches narrow to specific manufacturers and sources. Terms like "factory direct," "manufacturer," and "wholesale supplier" dominate. Buyers now click on B2B platforms, company websites, and technical support pages. **Stage 3 — Decision**: The final phase. Buyers search for transaction terms — pricing, minimum order quantities, shipping. They visit AiCaiGou, 1688.com, and supplier websites to place inquiries. The takeaway for advertisers: your search campaigns need to match the buyer's stage. A broad industry keyword and a transaction-focused keyword should lead to different landing pages and different calls to action. ### What Buyers Care About Most Across all industries, one factor dominates B2B purchase decisions more than any other: **price**. Chinese business buyers research pricing before anything else — before specifications, before supplier reputation, before delivery terms. But secondary concerns vary by industry: - **Building materials and metals**: word-of-mouth reputation, returns and repairs - **Machinery, electrical, and electronics**: resale and rental options - **All industries**: transaction channel reliability and payment security ### When and Where Buyers Search **Time patterns**: PC searches peak on workdays during office hours. Mobile searches are more evenly distributed — including weekends and evenings. This means B2B campaigns should run across the full week, with budget allocated to capture weekend mobile traffic that competitors often miss. **Regional patterns**: East China accounts for 50% of all B2B search demand. North China and South China each contribute around 12%, Central China 10%, with smaller shares from Southwest (5%), Northeast (5%), and Northwest (4%). Within South China, Guangdong province alone dominates the region's B2B activity.
"Demand is concentrated in East China, where economic activity is most intense. But different industries show different regional patterns — logistics and auto parts have outsized demand in Guangdong, Hubei, Sichuan, and Hebei, while consumer electronics demand peaks in Guangdong, Sichuan, and Henan." — 2020 Baidu B2B Industry Insights Report
## The B2B Platform Landscape: Who Is Winning Baidu's report segments B2B platforms into two models: - **Comprehensive platforms** (78% of search interest): 1688.com, AiCaiGou (Baidu's own platform), HC360.com — one-stop platforms with broad category coverage - **Vertical platforms** (22%): Industry-specific marketplaces with deeper domain expertise 1688.com leads in both search volume and brand recognition. But AiCaiGou is in the high-growth sweet spot — strong search share combined with rapidly rising awareness among business buyers. For overseas companies entering China, AiCaiGou represents a strategic opportunity because it is deeply integrated with Baidu's search ecosystem: your products show up not just on the marketplace, but in organic and paid search results across China's largest search engine. ## The 5-Step B2B Marketing Method: Baidu's Full-Funnel Approach The report outlines a structured approach for B2B companies to capture demand and convert it into sales on Baidu's platform: **Step 1: Precision Targeting** — Use search ads, brand zone, feed ads, and display to capture high-intent buyers at the exact moment they search for your product category. **Step 2: Knowledge Marketing** — Build authority through Baidu Baike entries, Baidu Zhidao answers, and short-form video content. Chinese B2B buyers rely heavily on these content sources during the awareness and comparison phases. **Step 3: Full-Funnel Website** — Use Baidu's Jimuyu (基木鱼) platform to build and host high-converting landing pages with built-in lead capture. No separate hosting or CMS needed. **Step 4: Private Domain Operations** — Use Baijiahao business accounts and live streaming to build a direct audience of repeat buyers. This is your owned channel within Baidu's ecosystem. **Step 5: Lead Operations** — Convert platform traffic into qualified leads through AiCaiGou's inquiry system, with real-time lead notifications and CRM integration. ## AiCaiGou: The Fastest-Growing B2B Platform You May Not Know About AiCaiGou (爱采购) is Baidu's own B2B procurement platform, and the report highlights several reasons it deserves attention from international brands: - **Baidu ecosystem integration**: Products listed on AiCaiGou appear in both organic and paid search results across Baidu — the equivalent of having your Amazon listings rank on Google - **Growing DAU and merchant base**: Daily active users and registered merchants have been trending up consistently - **Dual-scenario coverage**: Buyers reach your products through active search AND passive browsing recommendations - **Seasonal peaks**: Inquiry volume peaks in March-June and September-December, aligned with China's industrial procurement cycles - **Top regions**: Guangdong, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Shandong, and Hebei lead in platform usage
⚠️ Important: AiCaiGou's top 10 industry categories mirror the overall B2B search demand almost exactly — machinery, building materials, electrical, electronics, metals, chemicals, security, hardware, auto parts, and agriculture. If your product falls into one of these categories, you are competing in the highest-traffic B2B channel in China. Not being there means losing visibility to competitors who are.
## What This Means for Overseas B2B Companies The data from Baidu's report offers a clear strategic roadmap for international brands entering or expanding in China: 1. **Mobile-first campaigns are non-negotiable** — 70% of your buyers search on phones 2. **Price transparency wins** — Chinese B2B buyers research pricing before anything else 3. **East China first, then expand** — 50% of demand comes from the Yangtze River Delta region 4. **Cover all three search stages** — different keywords and landing pages for awareness, comparison, and decision 5. **Weekend mobile campaigns have hidden value** — less competition, real buyer intent 6. **AiCaiGou + Baidu Search is the strongest combination** — dual coverage across marketplace and search engine
