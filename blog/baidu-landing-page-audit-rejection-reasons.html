Baidu Landing Page Audit: Now Showing Exactly Why Your Page Was Rejected

💡 The Old Way (and Why It Wasted Your Time)

If you have ever submitted a landing page on Baidu's (the platform's built-in landing page builder), you know the frustration: it gets rejected, and all the system tells you is a vague text message like "non-compliant content."

No highlight. No pointer to the exact element. You are left guessing which image, which headline, or which piece of copy triggered the rejection.

For overseas teams without a Chinese-speaking operator, this is a weeks-long back-and-forth.

📊 What Changed (January 2021)

Baidu now highlights the exact rejection reason directly on the page preview. When a 基木鱼 page is rejected, you can hover over the "rejected" tag, and the system shows: 1. The number of violations on the page 2. Highlights of the specific elements that failed audit 3. Direct links to edit the problematic sections

💡 Why This Matters for Campaign Launch Speed

Every day your landing page sits in "rejected" status is a day you are burning budget on an underperforming page — or not spending at all.

💡 Pro Tip: Before submitting a 基木鱼 page for review, check that all images have proper licensing, all claims are substantiated, and there are no competing brand names in the copy. The most common rejection reasons are misleading claims and unlicensed copyrighted images.
