Baidu Ad Performance Diagnostic Tool: Find Out Why Your Campaign Suddenly Dropped

📊 The Panic Moment

It is 9 AM. You check your Baidu campaign dashboard. Your ad spend has dropped 40% overnight compared to last week. Impressions are down. Clicks are down. And you have no idea why.

For overseas advertisers managing Baidu campaigns remotely, this is a recurring nightmare. Is it a platform algorithm change? A competitor outbidding you? An account setting that got accidentally toggled? Without being able to read the Chinese-language reports, you are left guessing — and budget is burning while you guess.

📊 What Baidu Launched (July 2022)

Baidu's search promotion platform now has a full-traffic Abnormal Fluctuation Diagnostic Tool. Instead of manually hunting through reports, the tool analyzes your campaign and gives you a clear, structured answer in under 60 seconds.

🔍
4 Root Cause Categories
Account changes, competition, traffic, settings
⏱️
30-60 Seconds
Diagnosis completed in under a minute
📊
3-Stage Breakdown
Trigger → Bidding → Impression/Click
How it works:

1. You select a campaign or oCPC bid strategy that showed an abnormal fluctuation 2. Pick the anomaly date and a comparison date (fluctuation must be >20%, spend > ¥100, clicks >10) 3. Submit — within ~60 seconds, the tool delivers a full diagnosis

The three stages analyzed:
StageWhat It ChecksCommon Fix
Ad TriggeringWere your ads eligible for the auction? (targeting width, pass rate)Widen targeting; check negative keywords
Bid RankingDid your ads win the auction? (ad quality × bid strength)Increase bids; improve ad relevance
Impression & ClickDid users engage? (CTR trends)Refresh creative; test new copy

⏰ ⏰ When to Use Each Diagnostic Tool

Baidu now has a full suite of diagnostic tools. Knowing which one to use matters:

| Diagnostic Tool | Best For | |----------------|----------| | Impression Diagnosis (展现诊断) | Keywords with zero impressions | | Delivery Diagnosis (投放管理诊断) | Keywords with low impressions | | Abnormal Fluctuation (异常波动诊断) | Sudden drops or spikes in impressions/spend | | Keyword Quality Pre-check (质量度预检) | Pre-launch keyword-landing page relevance |

💡 Pro Tip: Run the diagnostic BEFORE you start making changes. The most common mistake is scrambling to adjust bids, keywords, and targeting simultaneously — which masks the original issue and makes future diagnosis harder.

📊 The Catch for International Brands

The diagnostic tool is inside Baidu's Chinese-language advertising platform. To use it effectively, you need: 1. A Baidu advertising account with sufficient data history 2. The ability to read Chinese diagnostic conclusions 3. The expertise to interpret the root cause analysis correctly

For overseas teams without a Chinese-speaking PPC manager, this tool generates valuable data that sits unread.
