How Baidu Feed Ads Work and Why They Matter

January 20254 min read
100M+
Daily Active Users on Baidu Feed

Baidu feed ads appear inside content streams on the Baidu App, homepage, and Tieba. Unlike traditional display advertising, they're targeted using search history, browsing patterns, and behavioral signals.

How Feed Ads Are Different from Search Ads

🔍 Search Ads

  • User types a specific query
  • Your ad appears based on keywords
  • High intent, immediate need
  • Best for bottom-of-funnel

📰 Feed Ads

  • User is browsing content
  • Your ad appears based on behavior
  • Discovery and consideration
  • Best for mid-funnel awareness

The Targeting Advantage

What makes Baidu feed powerful is the targeting data. Baidu knows what users searched for in the past, what content they consumed, and what topics they're interested in. Feed ads leverage this to show your brand to people who:

  • Have searched for related keywords in the past
  • Have browsed similar content on Baidu properties
  • Match your target demographic profile
  • Are located in your target geographic areas

When to Use Feed Ads

Feed ads excel when:

  • You want to reach users who aren't actively searching but fit your customer profile
  • You're building brand awareness before retargeting with search ads
  • You have a new product or service that users don't know to search for yet
  • You want to capture demand that exists but hasn't translated into active queries

Pair feed with search for a full-funnel strategy: feed builds awareness and captures discovery, search captures high-intent users ready to convert.
