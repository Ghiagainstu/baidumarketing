8 Proven Ways to Lower Your Baidu CPC Without Sacrificing Results

May 6, 2019 7 min read By Baidu PPC Pro Team

If you have run Baidu PPC for more than a few months, you have probably noticed the same trend: CPCs creeping up year after year. It is not your imagination — competition drives cost higher across every major Chinese ad platform.

"In Q1 2019 with the same level spending, the clicks were 20% less than Q1 2018. However, high CPC isn't 100% a bad thing. We brought better quality traffic — the leads number stayed the same." — Client case

One of our clients saw this firsthand. Same budget, year over year, 20% fewer clicks. The CPC had climbed 20%.

But here is the thing that surprised them: lead volume stayed flat. Higher CPC did not mean worse results — it meant the market had shifted and their account structure had not kept up.

Competition is not necessarily bad. It keeps you alert. But you do need practical tactics to keep CPCs under control. Here are eight that work.

1. Lower Your Bids (Strategically)

The most direct way to bring down CPC is to lower your bids. Start with the high-spend keywords that are driving up your average. You are not reducing everything across the board — identify the keywords where your current bid is above what the market requires, and bring those down first. A small adjustment on the top 10% of spenders can shift your account-level CPC noticeably.

⚠️
Watch the volume. Lowering bids can reduce impressions. Monitor your impression share after each change. If it drops more than 10%, the bid was probably correct where it was.

2. Shift to Long-Tail Keywords

Broad, generic keywords attract the most bidders. That drives up competition and CPC. Long-tail keywords have lower search volume but much clearer intent — and far less competition on Baidu.

Because long-tail keywords are more specific, they tend to earn higher Quality Scores. A higher Quality Score is the most effective way to lower CPC over the long term. The mechanism: Baidu charges less per click when your keyword relevance is high, because the platform wants to show users the most relevant result.

Focus on 3-5 word phrases that describe exactly what your customer is looking for. "Baidu advertising for foreign brands" will cost less per click than "Baidu advertising" and convert at a higher rate.

3. Use Geo Targeting by City Tier

Not all cities in China cost the same. Tier 1 cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen) have the highest CPCs because competition is fiercest and disposable income is higher. Tier 2, 3, and 4 cities can cost 30-50% less per click, depending on the industry.

Split your campaigns by city tier. Run separate ad groups for Tier 1 versus Tier 2-4. This does two things: it lowers your blended CPC, and it gives you per-city performance data so you can allocate budget to the regions that actually convert.

✅
Pro tip: If your product or service is relevant nationwide, running a Tier 2-4 campaign can significantly lower your overall cost structure while maintaining good conversion rates.

4. Use the Right Match Types

Baidu offers five match types: Exact, Phrase-Exact, Phrase-Synonym, Phrase-Core, and Broad. Each one controls how closely a search query must match your keyword before your ad shows.

Match Type Reach CPC Impact Best For
Exact Narrowest Lowest CPC, highest relevance High-converting keywords, tight budgets
Phrase-Exact Medium Moderate CPC Brand terms, proven converters
Phrase-Synonym Medium-Wide Higher CPC, more volume Expanding reach carefully
Phrase-Core Wide Higher CPC, less control Testing new keyword areas
Broad Widest Highest CPC, lowest relevance Avoid for limited budgets

For clients on limited budgets, we avoid Broad match entirely. We use Phrase match for brand and well-converting keywords, and Exact match for generic or low-performing terms where every click needs to count.

5. Apply Negative Keywords Religiously

Negative keywords are one of the most underused CPC-control tools on Baidu. They prevent your ads from showing on irrelevant queries that would otherwise inflate your costs and lower your CTR.

Pull your search term report at least once a month. Every time you do, you will find new irrelevant queries that triggered your ads. Add them to your negative keyword list. This is not a one-time setup — it is ongoing maintenance. Campaigns that skip this step see their CPC drift upward as Baidu's algorithm finds more ways to match your keywords.

6. Optimize for Mobile Traffic

For many industries, mobile CPCs on Baidu are lower than desktop. Not always — but often enough that it is worth testing. The catch: a mobile campaign is only as good as its landing page. If your mobile page loads slowly or is not optimized for small screens, even a low CPC will produce bad results.

Before launching mobile-specific campaigns, make sure your landing pages are mobile-friendly. Fast load time (under 3 seconds), thumb-friendly buttons, forms that work on a phone. Once the page is ready, create separate mobile campaigns with adjusted bids and see how they perform.

7. Increase Quality Score

Quality Score is directly correlated with CPC. Improve your score and your cost per click drops automatically, because Baidu rewards relevance with lower prices.

Three practical ways to raise Quality Score:

  • Update landing page content to match your keywords closely. A landing page about "industrial pumps" should not be generic — it should address the specific query behind each ad group.
  • Eliminate low-performing ad copies and replace them with fresh variations. Baidu's algorithm favors ads that earn clicks. Stale creatives drag down your score.
  • Use advanced ad styles — sitelinks, image extensions, call buttons. These improve CTR, and higher CTR directly improves Quality Score.

8. Apply Brand Protection

If you own a brand, apply for Baidu brand protection. This stops competitors from bidding on your brand terms. When competitors cannot use your brand name in their ad copy, their ad relevance and Quality Score drop — which reduces their ability to outbid you on terms that rightfully belong to your brand.

Brand protection is not automatic. You need to submit documentation proving trademark ownership. But once approved, it protects your brand CPC from competitive inflation and keeps your ad position secure for the queries that matter most to your business.

🎯
Bottom line: Brand protection is one of the few CPC controls that gives you a structural advantage rather than a temporary fix. If you qualify, it should be your first move.

📋 Key Takeaways

  • Lowering CPC is a means, not the goal. Never sacrifice lead quality for a cheaper click
  • Long-tail keywords + higher Quality Score = the most sustainable CPC reduction
  • Geo targeting by city tier lowers blended CPC and reveals regional performance data
  • Match type discipline (avoid Broad on limited budgets) keeps costs predictable
  • Negative keyword maintenance is a monthly habit, not a one-time setup
  • Brand protection gives structural protection against competitive CPC inflation

🤔 Not Sure Where to Start?

Eight tactics can feel overwhelming. The right approach depends on your account structure, your industry, and your data. What works for a B2B lead-gen campaign may not work for ecommerce, and vice versa.
