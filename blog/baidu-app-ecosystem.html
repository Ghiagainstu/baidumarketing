Baidu's App Ecosystem: Where Your Ads Actually Show Up

April 20254 min read

Baidu advertising runs across a stack of products that most foreign marketers haven't heard of. Here's the complete picture of where your ads can appear.

🔍 Baidu App

708M Monthly Active Users

The main search and feed surface. When most people talk about Baidu advertising, this is what they mean. Covers both search intent and feed discovery.

🗺️ Baidu Maps

3.4B Global POIs Covered

Captures local intent searches. If you have physical locations or serve specific geographic areas, this is essential for reaching nearby customers.

💬 Baidu Tieba

80M DAU, 23M Interest Forums

Reddit-like community platform. Good for niche targeting based on interests and communities. Often overlooked by international advertisers.

🎬 Haokan Video

80 min Average Session Time

Short-form video platform. High engagement, good for brand awareness and product demos. Video ads perform well here.

📚 Baidu Wenku

230M+ Users, 2.8B AI Interactions

Document sharing and professional content platform. Reaches a professional/education audience. Good for B2B targeting.

📦 Baidu Netdisk

1B Registered Users, 1hr Avg. Session

Cloud storage platform with massive reach. Often forgotten but offers extended session times and unique audience segments.

One Account, Multiple Surfaces

The beauty of Baidu's ecosystem is integration. You set up one Baidu marketing account, and you can activate advertising across all these surfaces with a single login. Your targeting data flows between platforms — search behavior informs feed delivery, geographic data from Maps enhances all placements.

This is different from Google, where YouTube, Search, and Display often require separate account structures and have limited data sharing between properties.

Which Platforms Should You Use?

For most international advertisers entering the Chinese market, the priority is:

  1. Baidu App (Search + Feed) — Your foundation. Highest volume, broadest reach.
  2. Baidu Maps — If you have local presence or serve specific regions.
  3. Haokan Video — If video content and brand awareness are priorities.
  4. Baidu Wenku — If you're targeting professionals or B2B audiences.
