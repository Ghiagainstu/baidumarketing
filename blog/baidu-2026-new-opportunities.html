1. Local Business: "Tailwind" Starts the Moment Customers Search

For local businesses, nothing is more frustrating than having potential customers within a 3-mile radius who can't find you online. When customers search "restaurant near me" or "dental clinic" on a maps app, does your business appear as just another pin on the map, or does it stand out as a trusted, premium storefront?

What's new: Baidu Maps Store Membership was fully upgraded in early 2025, with significantly lowered entry barriers. More small and medium businesses can now acquire customers through Maps — a core high-intent scenario — at a lower cost. Members get access to full-storefront decoration, search display priority, and exclusive data reports, transforming Maps from a "navigation tool" into a "decision and transaction entry point."

How to use it

  • Decorate your online storefront: Upon joining, you get an exclusive V badge and golden background. Upload high-resolution cover images, professional team photos, and service videos to create a polished online presence. A well-designed page builds professionalism and trust the moment a customer searches.
  • Create a clear path to visit: Add coupons and vouchers to stimulate purchase intent. Insert a one-tap "call now" button so users can contact you directly from the Maps page, or tap to navigate to your location. These intuitive touchpoints effectively drive foot traffic.

Best for: Industries that rely on local customers and trust-building — home renovation, locksmith services, auto services, and all local service businesses.

Case study: Dental clinic in Guangzhou

After joining Baidu Maps Store Membership, a dental clinic in Guangzhou transparently displayed its credentials, pricing, and case studies online. This dramatically lowered the barrier to decision for potential patients. In just one month, the storefront received over 300 targeted exposures, attracted 54 navigation-to-visit conversions, and generated 75 phone leads — successfully transitioning from traditional door-to-door marketing to precision online customer acquisition.
