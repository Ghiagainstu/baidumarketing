Baidu Advertising for International Brands: Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Edition)

Account Setup

Do I need a Chinese company to advertise on Baidu? Not necessarily. There are two routes: Route 1 — Direct opening: If you apply directly to Baidu as a foreign entity, you will be asked for a mainland Chinese business license (营业执照). This route is designed for domestically registered companies. Route 2 — Through an authorized partner (recommended): Baidu's service agreement explicitly supports agency participation. Through an authorized Baidu partner, foreign companies can open an advertising account using their overseas business registration documents — a US LLC, UK Ltd, EU GmbH, or equivalent — without needing a Chinese business license. Certain regulated industries (finance, healthcare, education, food) may face additional documentation requirements, but for most B2B and consumer brands this is a straightforward path. How BPP handles it: We work within Baidu's authorized partner framework to open the account under your foreign company's name. You retain 100% ownership and full admin access. No Chinese entity setup required on your end. What else do I need besides the business license?

A mainland Chinese bank account for billing, a Chinese phone number for account verification, and a registered local entity or partner who can hold the account. BPP provides all of this as part of our managed service.

How long does it take to set up a Baidu account?

With BPP handling the process, account setup typically takes 7 to 14 business days. This includes account registration, compliance review, and initial campaign configuration. If you were to do it independently — assuming you have the required credentials — expect 2 to 4 weeks with potential delays at each step.

Budget & Costs

What is the minimum budget to start advertising on Baidu?

Baidu's minimum initial deposit is approximately ¥2,400 (roughly $330 USD). From there, daily campaign budgets can start as low as ¥100 to ¥300 (approximately $14 to $42) for testing. BPP recommends a minimum 3-month budget to allow sufficient time for campaign optimization and meaningful data collection.

What does a realistic ROI timeline look like?

Most BPP clients begin to see stable lead costs within 4 to 8 weeks of campaign launch. The learning phase for oCPC bidding — where the AI optimizes for your conversion goals — requires at least 20 conversions per week to function effectively. Industries with higher search intent (B2B industrial, specialty manufacturing) tend to see faster ROI than high-competition consumer categories.

How does Baidu pricing compare to Google Ads?

CPCs on Baidu are typically lower than Google Ads for equivalent commercial-intent keywords, especially in B2B categories. However, the minimum deposit is higher. BPP clients in B2B sectors often see cost per lead 30% to 50% lower on Baidu compared to their Google Ads campaigns targeting comparable markets.

Platform & Capabilities

What is oCPC and why does it matter for my campaigns?

oCPC (Optimized Cost Per Click) is Baidu's AI-driven bidding system. It evaluates each click's conversion probability in real time and adjusts bids accordingly — raising bids on likely converters, lowering them on unlikely ones. For campaigns with sufficient conversion data (20+ conversions per week), oCPC typically delivers 20% to 40% lower cost per acquisition compared to manual CPC bidding. This matters because Baidu's 2026 platform upgrade has made AI bidding the default, not the exception.

Can I target specific cities or regions in China?

Yes. Baidu supports geographic targeting down to individual cities. This is particularly useful for B2B brands targeting industrial clusters — Guangdong for manufacturing, Shanghai for finance, Shenzhen for technology. Campaign budgets only spend against traffic from your selected regions.

How is Baidu different from Google Ads?

Five structural differences matter for international brands:
1. Credential requirements (Chinese entity required)
2. Language (entire dashboard in Chinese)
3. Ad formats (more formats per search result page)
4. AI bidding maturity (oCPC is more advanced than Google's Smart Bidding in the Chinese market context)
5. Compliance is stricter and more opaque from an overseas perspective

What is AiCaiGou and should I use it?

AiCaiGou is Baidu's B2B marketplace and lead generation platform. It functions as a procurement discovery engine — Chinese businesses use it to find suppliers for machinery, components, raw materials, and industrial products. If you are a B2B brand selling to Chinese businesses, AiCaiGou is worth considering. BPP can set up and manage your AiCaiGou presence alongside your search advertising account.

Can I run Baidu campaigns from overseas myself?

Technically possible if you have all the credentials, a Chinese-speaking team, and time to learn the platform. Practically, most international brands find the language barrier, time zone difference, and compliance complexity make self-management unsustainable. This is why most overseas companies work with a managed service provider like BPP.

Compliance & Risk

What compliance issues should international brands watch for?

Three areas in particular:
1. Industry licensing: Healthcare, finance, education, and food products require additional permits
2. Ad content review: Baidu's content review is stricter than Google's and more opaque; machine-translated ads frequently fail
3. Landing page requirements: Landing pages must follow Chinese web regulations, including ICP filing and content standards

BPP handles compliance as part of our managed service — you do not need to navigate this alone.

What happens if my account gets flagged or suspended?

Baidu accounts can be suspended for compliance violations ranging from unapproved ad content to landing page issues. Recovery often requires submitting documentation in Chinese and working through Baidu's support system. BPP manages this process for our clients, typically resolving routine flags within 3 to 5 business days.
