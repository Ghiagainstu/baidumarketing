Baidu Brand Zone Adds Material Pre-Review: Catch Compliance Issues Before Submission

📅 Jul 14, 2021 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ By Baidu PPC Pro Team

Baidu Brand Zone is the premium ad placement for your brand name on Baidu search results. It gives you a large branded takeover at the top of the page — essential for brand protection in China.

Baidu just added a time-saving feature: automatic material pre-review. Here is what it does and why it matters for overseas advertisers.

📊 What Changed

Previously, when you submitted Brand Zone materials for review, the first feedback on compliance issues came days later — after formal review. If the materials were rejected, you fixed them and resubmitted. That cycle could repeat.

Now, when you click "Save" or "Save and Submit Review," the platform instantly scans for compliance issues and shows a popup warning.

💡 What Pre-Review Catches
The system currently detects false or exaggerated claims in your materials. Coverage will expand to other violation types over time.

🔧 How It Works

  1. Create your Brand Zone materials
  2. Click "Save" or "Save and Submit"
  3. Pre-review runs automatically — if issues found, a dialog shows the flagged items
  4. Fix flagged items now, or ignore and submit anyway (advisory only)
  5. If you think the flag is wrong, use the "Feedback" button on the creation page

The pre-review is advisory, not blocking. You can submit materials with unresolved warnings. But Baidu recommends fixing them first to avoid repeated resubmissions.

📊 What It Does Not Cover

Baidu is clear about the limitations:

  • Current scope: Only false/exaggerated descriptions
  • Not a full review: Formal review still checks items not in pre-review
  • Not for every account: Some high-quality, pre-approved accounts may not trigger pre-review

🎯 Key Points

  • ✅ Catches exaggerated claims before formal review — saves resubmission cycles
  • ⚠️ Advisory only — you can ignore flags and submit anyway
  • ⚠️ Not comprehensive — formal review still catches additional issues
  • ℹ️ Feedback button available for disputing incorrect pre-review flags

💡 Why Brand Zone Matters for Overseas Advertisers

Brand Zone is one of the most important ad products for international brands on Baidu:

  • Protects your brand name from competitors bidding on it
  • Ensures your branded search results are controlled by you, not a competitor or counterfeit seller
  • Combines visual dominance (logo, images, multiple links) with the top position

Without Brand Zone, your brand name on Baidu is unguarded territory.

📊 The Compliance Challenge

Brand Zone materials go through Baidu's formal ad review — which is stricter than standard search ads. Common rejection reasons for overseas brands:

  • Claims not backed by Chinese-language documentation
  • Translated slogans that read as exaggerated in Chinese
  • Missing required industry certifications
  • Landing pages not compliant with Chinese regulations

Pre-review catches some of these upfront. But it does not replace understanding Baidu's ad policies.
