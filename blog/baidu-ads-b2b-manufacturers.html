"Baidu Ads for B2B Manufacturers: A Complete Playbook"

📅 2026-06-21 · ⏱️ 9 min · 📂 Strategy · ✍️ "Baidu PPC Pro Team"

If you sell industrial equipment, raw materials, components, or custom manufacturing services — and you want buyers in China to find you — Baidu is where they're looking. Not Google. Not Alibaba. Baidu.

This is a playbook for overseas B2B manufacturers who want to generate qualified leads through Baidu advertising. No theory. Just what works, what it costs, and where the traps are.

🏭 Why Baidu for B2B Manufacturing?

China's procurement behavior is different from the West. In most markets, a buyer starts with Google. In China, they start with Baidu — and for industrial purchases, they often go straight to Baidu's dedicated B2B platform.

Three things matter for B2B manufacturers on Baidu:

  1. Search intent is high. Someone searching "注塑机厂家" (injection molding machine manufacturer) or "不锈钢法兰价格" (stainless steel flange price) is already in buying mode. These aren't casual browsers — they're procurement managers with a PO waiting to be written.
  2. Competition is lower than you'd expect. Most foreign manufacturers don't advertise on Baidu. That means CPCs for industrial keywords are often ¥1–5 ($0.14–$0.70), compared to ¥10–30+ for consumer goods. The cost per qualified lead in B2B manufacturing can be 60–80% lower than equivalent Google Ads campaigns in other markets.
  3. Baidu has a dedicated B2B marketplace. 百度爱采购 (Baidu B2B / Aicgou) is a built-in wholesale sourcing platform directly integrated into Baidu search results. When someone searches for industrial products, listings from Aicgou appear above organic results. It's like having an Alibaba storefront inside Google — if Google had one.

📢 The Two Channels You Need to Know

Channel 1: Baidu Search Ads (搜索推广)

This is the standard PPC channel — text ads that appear at the top and bottom of Baidu search results. You bid on keywords, pay per click, and send traffic to your landing page.

For B2B manufacturing, this works best for:

  • Product-specific searches: "CNC加工中心" (CNC machining center), "液压缸规格" (hydraulic cylinder specifications)
  • Solution searches: "自动化生产线方案" (automated production line solutions)
  • Brand + product: "[Your brand] + 产品名" — protect your brand terms from competitors

Typical CPC range: ¥1–8 ($0.14–$1.10) for most industrial keywords. Highly competitive categories (e.g., packaging machinery, automation equipment) can hit ¥15–20 ($2.10–$2.80).

Channel 2: Baidu Aicgou (百度爱采购)

Aicgou is Baidu's own B2B marketplace — think of it as a hybrid between Alibaba and Google Shopping, but built into search results. Manufacturers list their products, and when a buyer searches, Aicgou listings appear in a dedicated carousel.

Key facts:

  • Annual membership: ¥6,980/year (~$960) for a basic store
  • No per-click cost for organic Aicgou listings (you pay for the membership, not per click)
  • Paid promotion is also available within Aicgou for higher visibility
  • Lead generation: Buyers can send inquiries directly through the platform

For overseas manufacturers, Aicgou has a catch: you typically need a Chinese business entity to open a store. This is where a service provider like BPP becomes essential — we handle the registration on your behalf.

🎯 Keyword Strategy for Industrial Manufacturers

The biggest mistake foreign manufacturers make on Baidu: translating their Google keywords directly into Chinese. This doesn't work because Chinese search behavior uses different terminology, and direct translations often miss the terms Chinese buyers actually use.

The Three-Tier Keyword Framework

Tier 1 — Product keywords (highest intent, highest cost) These are exact product searches by buyers who know what they want.

Examples:

  • "注塑机" (injection molding machine)
  • "数控车床" (CNC lathe)
  • "工业机器人" (industrial robot)
  • "激光切割机价格" (laser cutting machine price)

Tier 2 — Problem/Solution keywords (mid intent, moderate cost) These are searches by buyers who have a problem but haven't chosen a solution.

Examples:

  • "如何提高注塑效率" (how to improve injection molding efficiency)
  • "自动化包装线方案" (automated packaging line solution)
  • "工厂节能改造" (factory energy-saving renovation)

Tier 3 — Industry/Informational keywords (lower intent, low cost) These capture early-stage researchers and build awareness.

Examples:

  • "2026中国制造业趋势" (2026 China manufacturing trends)
  • "工业4.0标准" (Industry 4.0 standards)

Start with Tier 1. For most B2B manufacturers, 80% of your budget should go to Tier 1 keywords. They convert at 3–5× the rate of Tier 2, and the CPC is still low for industrial terms.

Match Type Strategy

Baidu offers three match types: exact (精确匹配), phrase (短语匹配), and broad (广泛匹配). For B2B manufacturing:

  • Use exact match for your top 20 product keywords
  • Use phrase match for solution keywords
  • Avoid broad match — it will burn your budget on irrelevant searches

One Baidu-specific trick: use negative keywords aggressively. Baidu's broad match is looser than Google's. Add negatives for:

  • 招聘 (jobs/recruitment)
  • 培训 (training)
  • 学校 (school)
  • 维修 (repair) — unless you offer repair services
  • 二手 (used/secondhand)

🖥️ Landing Pages That Convert Chinese Buyers

Your landing page is where most foreign manufacturers fail. A translated version of your English website won't work. Chinese B2B buyers have different expectations:

Must-Have Elements

  1. Chinese-language content — not just translated, but localized. Use terms that Chinese engineers and procurement managers use, not literal translations.
  2. Trust signals prominent above the fold:
  • Company establishment year
  • Factory photos (real ones, not stock)
  • Certifications (ISO, CE, SGS — with Chinese explanations)
  • Number of clients served or units delivered
  1. Inquiry form with WeChat option. Chinese B2B buyers prefer WeChat over email for initial contact. Include both options, but make WeChat prominent.
  2. Fast loading on China networks. If your site is hosted outside China, it will load slowly — sometimes 8–15 seconds on mainland networks. Use a China CDN or at minimum a Hong Kong/Asia-Pacific server. Anything over 3 seconds loses 53% of visitors.
  3. Mobile-responsive design. Over 60% of Baidu searches happen on mobile. Your landing page must work perfectly on a phone screen.

Common Mistakes

  • Using your English website as the landing page. Baidu will reject it during review, and even if it passes, conversion rates will be near zero.
  • No phone number visible. Chinese B2B buyers expect to see a phone number (Chinese format: +86-xxx-xxxx-xxxx). It's a trust signal, even if they don't call.
  • Missing ICP filing. If your site is hosted in mainland China, it needs an ICP filing (备案). Foreign-hosted sites don't need one, but load speed suffers.
