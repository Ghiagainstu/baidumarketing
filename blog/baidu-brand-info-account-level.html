Baidu Moves Brand Info to Account Level: Why It Matters for Ad Relevance and CTR

Brand information in Baidu search ads — the brand name displayed on every ad — is not cosmetic. It is a relevance factor that Baidu uses to match your ads to user intent. When the displayed brand name does not match what the user needs, CTR drops.

Baidu is changing how brand information is managed. Starting December 13, 2024, brand information is moving from unit and plan level to the account level.

Here is what changed and why getting it right matters.

📊 What Changed

📌
Before
Brand info set per unit or plan — inconsistent across campaigns
🎯
After
One default brand set at account level — uniform across all units

1️⃣ Timeline

| Scenario | What Happens | |---|---| | New accounts (after Dec 13) | Set a default brand at account level. All units use it automatically. | | Existing accounts (before Dec 13) | Unit and plan-level brands still work for ~2 months, then switch to account default. | | No default set | Unit/plan-level brands continue until you set a default. Then all switch. |

2️⃣ Cross-Platform Unification

Brand information is now shared between Baidu's traditional search platform and the newer LightShip (轻舸) platform. Set it once — both platforms use it.

3️⃣ New Brand Naming Rules

The naming rules for brand information are now more flexible:

  • ✅ Use your main business name, actual website name, or APP name
  • ✅ Regional restrictions removed — you can tailor brand names for cross-region campaigns
  • ✅ More natural brand names → better ad relevance → higher CTR

💡 Why Brand Information Matters

Brand information directly affects ad performance:

> "When the brand name displayed in an ad does not match user needs, willingness to click drops. When the brand registration region does not match the actual promotion region, users perceive the service as irrelevant." — Baidu

This is not about legal registration. It is about perceived relevance at the moment of search.

💡 Brand Info = Relevance Signal

Baidu uses your displayed brand name to assess ad-to-user relevance. Wrong brand name → lower relevance score → lower CTR → higher CPC. Getting it right is a direct cost optimization move.

📊 For Overseas Advertisers

Three implications:

1. Account structure matters more. If you have multiple product lines with different brand identities, they need separate Baidu accounts. Brand information is one-per-account now. 2. Brand naming in Chinese is not obvious. Your English brand name may not be the most relevant Chinese brand name for ad purposes. The new flexible naming rules help, but you need someone who understands both your brand and the Chinese search landscape. 3. Legacy cleanup window. If your existing Baidu account has inconsistent brand names across units, you have roughly two months to unify them. After that, the system will impose the default.
