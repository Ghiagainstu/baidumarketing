Baidu's Merchant Agent Just Became a Full AI Marketing Assistant

Jun 13, 2026 5 min read Platform By Baidu PPC Pro Team

On June 13, 2026, Baidu quietly dropped one of its most significant platform upgrades of the year. The Merchant Agent (商家智能体) has been rebuilt into a full-chain AI marketing assistant.

This is not a minor tweak. It adds five entirely new capabilities that cover the entire marketing funnel — from creative generation to performance analysis.

🔄 What Changed

FunctionWhat it does
Smart CreativeAuto-generates ad copy, images, and landing pages
Multi-Modal ContentCreates text + image + video from one brief
Smart BiddingAutomates bids, budget, and targeting
Real-Time InsightsDetects anomalies and suggests fixes
24/7 SupportAI customer service + human handoff

💡 Why This Matters

For overseas companies, this upgrade addresses three persistent pain points:

  • Creative in Chinese: The AI generates Chinese ad copy from an English brief — no local copywriter needed
  • Bidding complexity: Real-time automated bidding, budget reallocation, and targeting refinements
  • Performance analysis: Automatic anomaly detection and attribution without a dedicated data team

🔗 The Bigger Picture

This upgrade connects to Baidu's MEG restructuring, Baidu Yijing digital humans, and the Q1 AI revenue milestone. The Merchant Agent is becoming the central interface of Baidu's unified AI marketing stack.

🎯 What You Can Do Today

  • Find the Merchant Agent under "Tools" in your Baidu ad account
  • Test smart creative — provide a brief in English for Chinese ads
  • Enable automated bidding as a pilot campaign
  • Set up CPA and conversion monitoring alerts
