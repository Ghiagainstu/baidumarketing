Baidu PPC Terms Explained: Quality Score, Ranking, Bidding, and CPC

Jan 9, 2019 7 min read By Baidu PPC Pro Team

New Baidu advertisers often encounter terms that sound familiar from Google Ads but work differently in practice. Quality Score, ranking, bidding price, and CPC are the four most important concepts to understand before you launch your first campaign. Getting them wrong means overspending. Getting them right means lower costs, better positions, and higher ROI.

This guide breaks down each term using plain language and real examples so you can navigate the Baidu PPC dashboard with confidence.

🎯 What Is Quality Score?

Quality Score is Baidu's rating of how relevant your keywords, ad copy, and landing page are to the users who see your ads. Think of it as Baidu's answer to the question: "Does this ad serve our users well?"

A higher Quality Score means better display positions, higher rankings, and — importantly — a lower CPC. Under identical bidding conditions, a higher quality score can dramatically reduce what you pay per click and improve where your ad appears on the search results page.

Quality Score is not a vanity metric. It directly impacts your ad position and cost. A higher QS means better placement at a lower price — the single most effective lever for improving campaign efficiency.

📋 What Determines Quality Score?

Baidu's Quality Score is based on four factors:

FactorWeightWhat It Measures
CTRHighHow often users click your ad. Higher CTR means higher user interest and relevance.
Keyword-Ad RelevancyHighHow closely your ad copy matches the user's search query and the keyword.
Ad-Landing Page RelevancyHighWhether the landing page delivers on what the ad promises.
Account PerformanceMediumOverall performance of all your keywords, including quality scores of other campaigns.
💡
Tip: Don't neglect keywords with low quality scores. Every keyword contributes to your account's comprehensive performance score. Optimizing underperformers improves your entire account's standing, which in turn lifts the performance of your well-performing keywords too.

⚡ How to Improve Quality Score

✍️

Write Relevant Ad Copy

Include the target keyword in your headline and description. The ad must directly address the user's search intent.

📂

Group Keywords by Theme

Don't stuff 50 unrelated keywords into one ad group. Each group should share a common search intent.

🌐

Optimize Landing Page

The landing page must deliver on the ad's promise. A mismatch between ad copy and page content is the fastest way to tank your QS.

⏸️

Pause Low Performers

Keywords with consistently low CTR and high cost drag down your entire account average. Pause them or tighten match types.

💡
QS Impact: Two advertisers bidding the same amount on the same keyword can pay very different CPCs. The one with QS 10 might pay ¥2.50 per click while the one with QS 5 pays ¥5.00 — a 2x difference caused entirely by Quality Score. Investing time in QS optimization is the highest-ROI activity in Baidu PPC management.

📊 What Is Ranking (Ad Position)?

Ranking refers to where your ad appears among the paid search results. Historically, Baidu displayed ads on both the left side (top 5 positions) and right side of the search results page. Since mid-2016, Baidu has removed all right-side ad positions. Today, all paid results appear on the left, and the number of ad slots is limited.

The ranking formula is straightforward:

This means that Ranking = Quality Score × Bidding Price. Baidu uses this product to determine which ads appear in which order:

  • If two advertisers have the same Quality Score, the one with the higher bid gets the better position.
  • If two advertisers have the same bid, the one with the higher Quality Score gets the better position.
  • Baidu's system continuously adjusts rankings based on real-time data.

A better ranking brings more attention from searchers. Users naturally scan top-to-bottom, so the #1 position typically receives the highest CTR, which in turn feeds back into a higher Quality Score — creating a virtuous cycle.

💰 What Is Bidding Price?

Your bidding price is the maximum amount you are willing to pay for a single click on your ad. This is a ceiling, not the actual amount you pay.

⚠️
Critical distinction: Your bidding price is NOT your actual CPC. Think of it as the maximum bid you authorize. The actual CPC is calculated dynamically through the auction and is always equal to or less than your bid.

Bidding is one of several factors that affect your ranking, but it is not the only one. A lower bid combined with a high quality score can outrank a higher bid with a low quality score. This is why optimizing Quality Score is often more cost-effective than simply raising your bids.

💲 What Is CPC (Cost Per Click)?

CPC is the actual price you pay when a user clicks your ad. Unlike your bid — which is a fixed amount you set — CPC is a dynamic number calculated based on the real-time auction conditions at the moment of the search.

🔬
Insight: CPC is a dynamic metric. If your competitors raise their bids or improve their quality scores, your CPC can increase even if you haven't changed anything. Regular account monitoring and QS optimization are not optional — they are required to maintain cost efficiency.

🔢 Putting It All Together: A Worked Example

Here is how the three terms interact in a real scenario:

AdvertiserQuality ScoreBidRanking (QS × Bid)Actual CPC
Advertiser A9¥5.0045 (Rank #1)Calculated: (4 × 10) / 9 + 0.01 = ¥4.45
Advertiser B10¥4.0040 (Rank #2)Calculated: (5 × 7) / 10 + 0.01 = ¥3.51
Advertiser C7¥5.0035 (Rank #3)Calculated: ¥2.00 (min. display price)

Notice that Advertiser A has the top ranking with a high Quality Score and bid, but their actual CPC (¥4.45) is determined by Advertiser B's QS and CPC. Advertiser B has the highest QS (10) and the lowest actual CPC (¥3.51) — despite bidding less than A.

This illustrates why improving Quality Score is almost always a better strategy than simply raising bids.

✅ Key Takeaways

  • Quality Score = how relevant your ads are. Higher QS = lower CPC + better position.
  • Ranking = QS × Bid. Both factors matter equally.
  • Bidding price = your maximum CPC (a ceiling). The actual cost is always lower.
  • Actual CPC = (next CPC × next QS) / your QS + 0.01. It is dynamic.
  • Improving QS is more cost-effective than raising bids to win higher positions.
  • Monitor and optimize your account regularly — competitor changes affect your CPC.
